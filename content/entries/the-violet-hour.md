## Broadcast log

At 1:13 a.m., the station cut to dead air. At 1:14 a.m., callers started insisting the host had named names. By sunrise, the engineer denied the broadcast happened at all. The only surviving artifact is a cassette with a handwritten label: violet hour, take two.
