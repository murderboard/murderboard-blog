## Opening note

On paper, the call looked routine: one body, one alley, one witness who should not have been there. But the witness was out before sunrise every day with six dogs and an opinion about every block in Rittenhouse. She knew which deliveries arrived late, which windows stayed lit, and which strangers kept pretending to belong.

What she found beside the body was stranger than the blood: pages from a manuscript nobody could identify, typed on heavyweight cream stock, annotated in two hands.
